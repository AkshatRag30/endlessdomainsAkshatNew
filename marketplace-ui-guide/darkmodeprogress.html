Primitives migrated to tokens

Built and checked, not committed

9 October 2026 · branch dark-mode · not committed (held on request)

Every colour in the primitives that should change between light and dark now reads from a role token. Brand fills, glows, shadows and decorative gradients look the same in both themes, so they were left as they are. The 10 dark icons that would have vanished on black are now drawn in currentColor. With the team's decision to map near shades to their role, light mode shifts slightly in places; every shift is listed below.

How it was done

  • A written rulebook decided each colour by what it does (card fill, text, divider, status, chart, brand), not just its value. White as a card fill became the surface role; white text on a blue button stayed white.
  • The primitives were split into four groups (buttons; cards and toast; badges, banners, tooltip and modal; inputs, toggles, charts and the rest) and migrated in parallel against the same rulebook, then reviewed together.
  • One exception to the near shade rule: the disabled price input text uses the secondary role, because the disabled role would have made it visibly lighter (#7c7c7c to #b2b2b2).

    How to test

    1. Add NEXT_PUBLIC_DARK_MODE=true to .env and restart npm run dev (port 3002). The flag is read at build time, so a restart is required.
    2. The toggle appears right of Connect Wallet in the header.
    3. Click it. In DevTools the <html> tag should flip between data-theme="light" and data-theme="dark".
    4. Reload while dark. The knob should already be on the moon with no flash.
    5. Remove the flag and restart. The toggle disappears and the site is always light.

    Until phase 4, only the toggle, native scrollbars and form controls and the mobile address bar change. Page colours stay light.
