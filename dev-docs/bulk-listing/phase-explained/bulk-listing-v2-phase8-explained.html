Bulk listing v2 · phase 8 explained

Phase 8 in simple words: delisting for real

Until now, Delist only hid a listing on our site; it could still be bought on the blockchain. Phase 8 makes Delist cancel the listing on the blockchain, lets you delist many at once, shows "Cancelling" on the row, and fixes names blocked by an older, cheaper listing. What every file does, why, and how to try it. Written 23 Nov 2026; line numbers as of that day.

1. The story in six sentences

  1. A listing is a signed message. Our old Delist only told our backend "stop showing it"; the signed message stayed valid on the blockchain until it expired.
  2. So anyone with a copy could still buy the name at the old price, and the backend refused to let you relist higher (your 3 refused names).
  3. Mohit sir's F7 says: delist on the blockchain, with one Seaport cancel transaction for any number of listings.
  4. Phase 8 does exactly that for the Delist button, adds "Bulk delist" for many at once, and adds a button to cancel the older listings that block a relist.
  5. The blockchain takes about a minute to confirm, so My Domains shows "Cancelling" until it does, and "Cancel not confirmed" with Cancel again if it fails (you chose this instead of a separate My Listings page).
  6. Cancelling costs a tiny network fee in POL; listing stays free.

2. Words to know first

WordIn simple words
Off chain delist (old)Only our backend forgets the listing. Free, but the listing can still be bought on the blockchain.
On chain cancel (new)A transaction to Seaport saying "these orders are dead". After it, Seaport refuses them forever. Costs a small fee.
Signed order (rawOrder)The exact listing you signed. Seaport needs it back to know what to cancel. Our backend keeps it.
Order hashA listing's fingerprint. We check the signed order matches it before cancelling.
Transaction hashThe receipt number of the cancel transaction. We give it to the backend, which watches the blockchain for it.
Cancel pending / CancellingSent, but the backend hasn't seen the blockchain confirm it yet (usually about a minute).
Cancel not confirmedNo confirmation after 30 minutes, or the transaction failed. The listing is hidden but can still be bought, so cancel again.
Older, cheaper listingAn old listing of the same name at a lower price that is still valid. The backend won't let you list higher while it exists.

6. The rules · src/helpers/my-domains/onChainCancel.ts

2

The cancel recipe

New · 146 lines · pure
  1. runOnChainCancel(hashes, deps) · line 40 1. Remove duplicates.
    2. Prepare: wallet connected, on Polygon, wallet proof fresh.
    3. Get every signed listing from the backend.
    4. Check each one is really that listing (its fingerprint matches) and was made by your wallet.
    5. Send one Seaport cancel transaction for all of them.
    6. Tell the backend with the transaction's receipt number. If it says some were already sold or cancelled, tell it about the rest, with the same receipt number.
    If telling the backend fails for another reason, the cancel still happened on the blockchain; the backend notices on its own within a minute.
  2. pendingCancelsByToken(rows) · line 103From your recent cancels, keeps only the ones still waiting, one per name, with whether they're "not confirmed".
  3. cancelOutcomeText(outcome) · line 117Turns the result into the message you approved, like "Listing cancelled…" or "Not enough POL…".

13. Safety checks before any fee is paid

  • Your wallet must be on Polygon.
  • Every signed listing must match its fingerprint, so we never cancel the wrong thing.
  • Every listing must be yours: Seaport would refuse someone else's, and you'd still pay the fee.
  • If any check fails, nothing is sent and nothing is charged.

14. Questions you might have

  1. How much does a cancel cost?A small fee in POL on Polygon, usually a fraction of a cent to a few cents. MetaMask shows it before you confirm. One transaction covers up to 50 listings.
  2. Does listing cost anything now?No. Listing is still a free signature. Only delisting costs a fee.
  3. Why "Cancelling" and not just "Not listed"?The blockchain needs about a minute, and the backend confirms by watching it. Until then, the label tells you it's on its way.
  4. Why don't I always see the labels?Reading them needs a fresh wallet proof (under about an hour). We don't pop your wallet on page load just for that; right after a delist the proof is fresh.
  5. Can I cancel my old listings from before phase 8?Yes. The backend still has their signed copies, so Delist (if still shown) or the "Cancel older listings" box works for them.
  6. What about lowering a price?For now it's delist (small fee) then list again. Whether price cuts should stay free is Mohit sir's call (V7).
  7. Is there a My Listings page?No, you decided My Domains shows the states instead (option A).
