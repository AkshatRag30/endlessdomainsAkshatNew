Phase 5 in simple words: the drawer tells the truth

Phase 5 is Mohit sir's F4, F6 items 1 to 3 and the design issues in F8. Most of it makes sure what the drawer shows is exactly what gets signed, plus a Cancel button that really works. Written 23 Nov 2026; line numbers as of that day.

1. The story in five sentences

  1. Mohit sir's PDF lists things the drawer gets wrong or that can't work: a pricing card with no data behind it, a date from your computer's clock, a button you can't press, and some wording that overpromises.
  2. Seven of his 11 design issues were already fixed, so phase 5 did the rest (one goes to phase 7).
  3. You approved every wording change first on a drawn page.
  4. The biggest change you can feel is a real Cancel while the wallet is open: press it, and even if you approve in the wallet later, nothing is listed.
  5. The other changes keep the drawer's numbers and dates identical to the signed order.

3. How Cancel is safe

Only the wallet can close its own popup, so pressing Cancel can't take MetaMask's request away. What Cancel does is make the drawer stop caring about the answer. Each attempt gets a number; Cancel changes the number. When the wallet answers later, the code sees the number changed and throws the signature away before anything is posted.

If you were signing names one by one and some were already listed, those stay listed, and Cancel shows you which ones. Cancel is hidden while the batch is being posted, because that can't be called back.

4. Why the date moved 5 minutes

Every order starts 5 minutes in the past (so a slow block can't make it "not started yet") and runs for your chosen days from there. The form now shows exactly that end time, using Polygon's clock. That's why it reads 15:17 when you open it at 15:22. The result screen shows the time actually signed, which can be a minute later if you took a minute to click List.

7. Questions you might have

  1. Why is the result time a minute later than the form?The form shows the time when you opened it; the result shows the time actually signed, a minute later.
  2. Does Cancel close MetaMask?No, only the wallet can. You can reject or ignore it there; approving does nothing now.
  3. Where did the percentage option go?Removed, as Mohit sir's decision 5 says: there's no estimate data behind it.
  4. Is this all of Mohit sir's design issues?All but one: mixed chains in one batch is phase 7.
