1. The story in five sentences

  1. Before phase 8, lowering a price was free: delist (just hidden) and list again cheaper.
  2. Phase 8 made delist a real blockchain cancel, so that path now costs a small fee.
  3. Mohit sir's decision 1 says price cuts should stay free; only raises need the real cancel.
  4. So listed names now have an Edit price button: a lower price is one free signature, a higher price is refused with a pointer to Delist.
  5. Delist itself moved into the edit drawer, as a clear red "Remove this listing" box.

3. Why a free price cut is safe

A free cut hides the old listing on our site but doesn't cancel it on the blockchain, so the old, more expensive listing stays valid until it ends. That's fine: a buyer will always take the cheaper new one, and if anyone did pay the old price, you'd get more money.

A raise is the opposite: the old cheaper listing could still be bought, so the backend refuses a raise while it exists. That's why raising goes through a real Delist.

Because the old listing is hidden only after you've signed, cancelling the signature never leaves your name unlisted.

6. Where Delist went

  1. First: a grey "Delist instead" link under Sign new price. Too easy to miss.
  2. Options compared (wording/delist-placement-options.html). Two buttons on the row don't fit on laptops.
  3. Chosen: a red Remove this listing box in the drawer, right under the "Lowering the price is free" note, with a Delist button (one small fee).
  4. Bulk delist stays in Quick Actions.

8. Questions you might have

  1. Why does MetaMask show a smaller "Listing price"?It shows what you receive after the 2.5% fee (292.5 for a 300 listing).
  2. Can I raise the price for free?No. Delist first (small fee), then list again at the higher price.
  3. Is the old listing gone?From our marketplace, yes. On the blockchain it stays valid until it ends, but at a higher price nobody will choose.
  4. Is this what Mohit sir asked for?Yes, decision 1: price cuts free, increases on chain. The backend guide preferred on chain for everything, but keeps the free hide we use.
