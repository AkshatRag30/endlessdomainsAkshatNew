How it works

Now (phase 8)
To lower a price: Delist (a transaction, small fee) → List again (free signature).
After option B
To lower a price: Edit price → type a lower price → sign (free). Behind the scenes: after you sign, the old listing is hidden on the marketplace for free, and the new one goes live. No transaction, no fee.

Why it's safe: the old listing is more expensive, so even though it stays valid on the blockchain until it ends, nobody will pay more while the cheaper one is live (and if they did, you'd get more). Raising a price is different: the old cheaper one could still be bought, so raising still needs a real delist first.

Order of steps: sign the new price first, then hide the old one, then post the new one. If you cancel the signature, nothing changes and your listing stays as it was.
