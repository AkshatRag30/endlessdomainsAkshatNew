D. Stopping halfway

If you reject approval 2 after approval 1 went through, you go back to the approval screen, which now lists only the one still missing, with this toast:

Approval 2 of 2 was cancelled in your wallet. The first one is done and stays done.

Rejecting the first (or only) approval keeps today's toast: "Approval was cancelled in your wallet. Nothing was charged."

E. When the fee can't be estimated no change

If the gas estimate fails (a slow network), the row falls back to today's text, "A small network fee", instead of a made up number. If we can't tell whether you're approved at all, today's toast stays: "We couldn't check your approval status. Please try again."
