Each card below is one screen or message, drawn roughly as it will look, with when it appears and what changes from today. Colours and spacing will follow the real design system; this page is only about the words and where they go. Approve each part (A to G), or tell me what to change.

Why anything changes

Today
Delist only hides the listing on our site. It's free, but the listing can still be bought on the blockchain at the old price until it expires. That's why 3 of your names were refused last week.
After phase 8
Delist cancels the listing on the blockchain. Nobody can buy it after that. It costs a small network fee in POL (a fraction of a cent to a few cents), and MetaMask shows the fee before you confirm.

A. The Delist button on a row (one listing)

Where: My Domains rows and cards, and your own listings on the marketplace home page. The button itself still says Delist; what happens after the click is new.

Today
Click Delist → (sometimes a free wallet check) → toast "Listing removed."
After
Click Delist → toast below → MetaMask opens with a transaction (shows the fee) → you confirm → toast "Listing cancelled."

Where: My Domains, on the row of the name you just delisted. The blockchain takes about a minute to confirm; the page checks every 20 seconds.

2. Once confirmed: the label disappears and the row is a normal "Not listed" row.
