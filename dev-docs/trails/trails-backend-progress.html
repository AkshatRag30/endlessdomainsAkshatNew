What needs a fix

  1. A1, a failed attach is ignored (most important). If attaching a quote fails (server busy, network drop, login expired, or wallet verification older than 60 minutes, which can happen when prepare ran with a 54 minute old verification and the buyer paid 10 minutes later), the server never stores that quote. The buyer can still press Pay. The server then can't link the payment to the checkout, so neither the drawer nor the backend's background tracker sees it finish, and no email is sent. Suggested fix: retry the attach (for example 3 tries, 2 s apart); if it still fails, take the buyer off the pay step to a "Couldn't start the payment, nothing was charged" screen before they can pay, and for WALLET_NOT_VERIFIED verify again and re attach. Needs the user's go ahead (new screen behaviour).
  2. A2, failed shown too early. The document says failed can still become refunded. We give up after 20 minutes and say "didn't go through". Our text already says "If money left your wallet, Trails refunds it", so the buyer isn't misled, but the refund screen never appears if it comes later. Options: keep polling a failed checkout longer (the server tracks it anyway), or keep it as is and rely on the backend's refund email. Question for Mohit (Q28).
  3. A3, success without our sale record. Same as Q18, already with Mohit. No change until he answers.

Bug report for Mohit: same chain USDT quotes refused (17 Nov)

Written 17 Nov 2026 for Mohit sir after he forwarded the server's "intent mismatch" alert email. Subject: "Trails: same chain USDT quotes refused (verifier check 3)". Status: to be sent by the user; waiting on the backend's choice of fix. Linked: finding F9, problem P8, test T14.

1

What happens

  1. When the buyer picks USDT on Polygon in the Trails widget, POST /trails/checkouts/:id/intent returns 200 with status: "mismatch" and the drawer shows "Couldn't start the payment".
  2. Every time, for every buyer, with that token. Tokens on other chains (BNB on BSC, USDC on Base) attach fine.
  3. Each refusal sends the internal alert email. The ones Mohit received are from our tests with wallet 0x5b1c…A24E on swaparena.og (0.10 USDT): checkouts 47685799-0645-4797-9960-8da4ad00d58c, e799e978-8119-463d-8bb8-4e0f45c2be58, a947238c-78a3-4db3-b169-1d9bf450204a, 17 Nov 06:31 to 06:41 UTC.
  4. Nothing is paid; the frontend stops before payment.
2

Cause

  1. For a cross chain quote Trails sends destinationCalls with 3 calls (hydrate, execute and sweep wrapper), and the verifier is built for exactly that shape.
  2. For a same chain, same token quote (USDT on Polygon to USDT on Polygon) Trails skips the destination side: no destinationCalls, the 4 calls are in originCalls, and quote.routeProviders is empty. Confirmed with GetIntent on all three intents (0x9c64…a001, 0xd34e…5701, 0xd06d…5b01).
  3. So intent-verifier.ts fails check 3 in parse ("destinationCalls is not the three call shape"); it would also fail check 8 ("quote.routeProviders is missing").
  4. Prepare, price, buyer and our calls are all correct; the frontend works as intended.
3
