Backend bugs found (need a fix on the backend)

F9

USDT on Polygon quotes are always refused (verifier check 3)

  1. What happens: when the buyer picks USDT on Polygon in the widget, attach returns 200 { status: "mismatch", intentId: null }, the drawer shows "Couldn't start the payment", and the internal alert email goes out. Every time, every buyer. Nothing is paid.
  2. Your alert emails are from my tests with wallet 0x5b1c…A24E on swaparena.og (0.10 USDT): checkouts 47685799-0645-4797-9960-8da4ad00d58c, e799e978-8119-463d-8bb8-4e0f45c2be58, a947238c-78a3-4db3-b169-1d9bf450204a, 06:31 to 06:41 UTC.
  3. Cause: for a same chain, same token quote Trails sends no destinationCalls; its 4 calls are in originCalls and quote.routeProviders is empty (checked with GetIntent on all three intents: 0x9c64…a001, 0xd34e…5701, 0xd06d…5b01). intent-verifier.ts parse fails check 3 ("destinationCalls is not the three call shape"); check 8 ("quote.routeProviders is missing") would fail too.
  4. Why it matters: the widget often starts on USDT on Polygon for buyers who hold it, so they hit the error at once.
  5. Fix, option A: support the same chain shape in the verifier (verify originCalls with the same rules: one Seaport call with our fulfillCalldataHash, USDT approve before it, recipient and sweep to the buyer, EXACT_OUTPUT; empty provider list allowed only here).
  6. Fix, option B: refuse it up front with its own code (for example SAME_CHAIN_NOT_SUPPORTED), no alert email; the drawer then says to use "USDT from your wallet" (new wording).
F1

A refunded payment can be saved as succeeded, with no refund email

  1. What happens: the same wallet buys a listing directly and also pays for it through Trails (the 12 Nov check 12). Trails refunds the Trails payment, but the checkout is saved as succeeded: the buyer sees "is yours" and no refund, no refund email is sent (onRefunded is skipped), and admin stats count a sale.
  2. Cause: toCheckoutState (trails-tracker.rules.ts:132) and toStateFromRow (trails-checkout-state.mapper.ts) mark success whenever a sale of the listing to that wallet exists (our old frontend rule 1, copied).
  3. Suggested fix: succeeded only when the sale's transaction is this checkout's destinationTxHash; otherwise keep Trails' status (refunded).

Notes on the API document (no backend change needed unless you say so)

  1. Resume list: the document suggests status=attached,executing. attached is an unpaid quote, so we use executing,failed for the banner and resume.
  2. /config timing: section 3 says call it when the drawer opens, section 6 says once in getCheckoutSource(). We read it on every drawer open.
  3. Price range: we hide the option for listings outside minPriceUsdt / maxPriceUsdt, so buyers never meet PRICE_OUT_OF_RANGE after choosing it.
  4. Slippage: we pass route.slippageTolerance (0.005) as asked, but the widget ignores it (its QuoteIntent has no slippage field, seen 12 Nov), so Trails uses Auto.
  5. CHECKOUT_IN_PROGRESS only appears once another buyer has paid (within 2 minutes); two buyers who only have a price open both get their own checkout.
