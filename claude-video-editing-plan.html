The reel spec, memorise it once

Put these numbers in your CLAUDE.md (week 3) so Claude never has to guess them.

Frame
1080 × 1920, 9:16 vertical
Frame rate
30 fps (shoot at 30 too, so nothing gets resampled)
Length
30 to 40 seconds; hook lands in the first 1.5 seconds
Video
H.264, yuv420p, around 8 Mbps, faststart
Audio
AAC 192 kbps, loudness around −14 LUFS, music sitting well under the voice
Captions
Inside the dashed box. Two to four words at a time, large, high contrast, the spoken word highlighted
Pacing
A visual change (cut, zoom, card, emphasis) every 2 to 4 seconds

DAY 3

FFmpeg basics, with Claude driving

Don't memorise FFmpeg. Ask Claude Code to do it, then read what it ran. You'll pick up the flags in a week.

Prompt for Claude Code
Inspect raw/day2.mp4 with ffprobe and tell me its resolution, fps, codec and duration. Then trim it to start 0.5s after the clap and end at the last spoken word, keeping it 1080x1920 at 30fps. Save to work/trimmed.mp4 and explain each flag you used in one line.
What it should roughly run
ffmpeg -ss 00:00:01.8 -to 00:00:39.5 -i raw/day2.mp4 \
  -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -preset medium -c:a aac -b:a 192k work/trimmed.mp4
DAY 4

Transcribe with word timings

Install and run Whisper locally
pip install -U openai-whisper
whisper work/trimmed.mp4 --model small --language en \
  --word_timestamps True --output_format json --output_dir work
Tip: the small model is a good speed to accuracy balance on a laptop. If tech jargon keeps coming out wrong, try medium.

DAYS 9 and 10

Cinematic word by word captions

Prompt for Claude Code
In this HyperFrames project, build a 1080x1920 composition that plays work/trimmed.mp4 full frame and shows captions from work/trimmed.json (Whisper word timestamps).
Captions: groups of 2 to 4 words, centred at about 62% height, inside Instagram's safe zone (clear of top 250px, bottom 420px, right 120px). Each word fades and rises 12px as it is spoken; the word being spoken is highlighted in #6B3BFF. Font: bold display sans, 72px, white with a soft dark shadow. Follow style.md for anything I haven't specified. Use GSAP on the composition timeline so it is fully seekable.
DAYS 11 and 12

Transitions and B roll cards

Accuracy: any number you put on screen should come from a source you can name in the caption or comments. In a jobs market niche, one wrong stat costs more trust than ten good reels earn.
DAYS 13 and 14

Sound: music, SFX and loudness

Duck music under voice, then normalise
ffmpeg -i out/reel.mp4 -i sounds/music.mp3 -filter_complex \
"[0:a]asplit=2[v1][v2];[1:a]volume=0.30[m];\
[m][v1]sidechaincompress=threshold=0.05:ratio=8:attack=20:release=300[d];\
[v2][d]amix=inputs=2:duration=first:normalize=0,loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5[a]" \
-map 0:v -map "[a]" -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k out/reel-mixed.mp4
Week 2 done when you've posted (or at least privately exported) one complete reel: trimmed, captioned, with transitions, a card and sound.

DAYS 15 and 16

Write your CLAUDE.md

Claude Code reads CLAUDE.md at the start of every session in that folder. Put your rules there once and every reel follows them.

reels/CLAUDE.md, a starting point
# Reel editing rules
Output: 1080x1920, 30fps, H.264 yuv420p ~8Mbps, AAC 192k, faststart, ~-14 LUFS.
Safe zone: no text in top 250px, bottom 420px, right 120px.
Captions: 2-4 words per group, display sans 72px bold, white, spoken word in #6B3BFF.
Pacing: a visual change every 2-4s (punch in, card, cut). Hook visible by 1.5s.
Transitions: subtle only. Crossfade 6-10 frames, zoom through, blur wipe.
Stats on screen: only numbers I give you, always with the source in a small label.
Sound: SFX on exact transition frames; music ducked under voice.
Folders: raw/ (camera files), work/ (trims, transcripts), out/ (renders), sounds/.
Template: compositions live in template/; copy, never edit, for each new reel.
Always: preview before full render; tell me the render time.

Your daily loop after the plan

About 30 minutes per reel once the template is mature.

5 min · Run the pipeline

10 min · Review edit.json

Fix the hook wording, choose emphasis words, fill in sourced numbers for cards.

10 min · Render and check

Preview the tricky moments, render, watch once on your phone.

5 min · Post

Caption with sources and any Openverse attribution, then schedule.

Things that will trip you up

Captions drift out of sync

Usually a frame rate mismatch. Shoot at 30, render at 30, and never let the clip get resampled to 29.97 or 60 halfway through.

Fonts change on render

The renderer only sees fonts you ship in the project. Add the font files; don't rely on what's installed on your laptop.

Animations jump when scrubbing

Something is running on a timer or CSS transition. Everything must hang off the composition timeline.

Music gets muted on Instagram

Only use Creative Commons tracks whose license allows your use, and credit them. Trending songs are copyrighted unless you add them through Instagram's own music picker.

Hitting Claude's usage limits

Reuse the template instead of regenerating compositions, and batch edits in one session. If you still hit limits daily, consider Max (₹11,999/mo).
